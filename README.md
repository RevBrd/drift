# DRIFT

**[Play it in your browser →](https://revbrd.github.io/drift/)**

Asteroids, except your gun is also a thruster. Every shot you fire shoves you the other way.
There are no brakes and almost no friction, so the only way to stop is to turn around and shoot
at nothing. Aiming and moving are the same decision.

Run out of fuel and the gun is all the steering you have left.

## Modes

| Key | Mode | |
|---|---|---|
| `1` | **DRIFT** | The real game. Fuel, weapon drops, hazards that ladder in as the waves climb, three ships. Every weapon you hold costs you score, and dying strips them all |
| `2` | **CLASSIC** | Just you and the rocks |
| `3` | **SURVIVAL** | No guns at all. One ship. Dodge for as long as you can |
| `4` | **RIDICULOUS** | One ship, weapons stack three deep, everything at once. Not a serious mode |

## The pulse

`Shift` (or `0`) fires the pulse, which clears everything near you. It is also unstable. Every
time you use it, and every time you die holding it, the chance it blows up in your hands goes up,
and it never comes back down. It also knocks your instruments out for a while, a little longer
each time. Use it when you have to.

## What's out there

In DRIFT and RIDICULOUS, broken rocks sometimes leave something behind. Fly into it to take it.

| | Pickup | |
|---|---|---|
| **F** | Fuel | Fills the tank. Thrust is the only thing that burns it |
| **R** | Rapid | Fire faster |
| **P** | Power | Heavier shots that cleave into nearby rocks |
| **T** | Twin | More barrels |
| **✦** | Pulse | Re-arms the pulse |
| **$** | Gem | Points, and nothing else |
| **↑** | Ship | One more life. In DRIFT, every 20,000 points earns one, carried by the next rock you break |

Each weapon you hold docks your score multiplier, and dying takes them all. Getting stronger
and scoring well pull against each other for the whole run.

The field doesn't stay simple. Rocks change first, and then things that aren't rocks turn up:

| Wave | Arrives |
|---|---|
| 3 | Big rocks |
| 5 | Iron rocks |
| 7 | Gravity well |
| 8 | Large iron rocks |
| 9 | Solar flare |
| 11 | Shielded rocks |
| 13 | Pulsar |
| 15 | Comet |

You'll find out what each one does when you meet it. CLASSIC and SURVIVAL have none of this:
there it's just you and the rocks.

Every run ends with a rank, from a ladder of famous fictional pilots. The top one is a secret.

## Playing

Desktop browser and a keyboard. There are no touch controls.

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `←` `→` / `A` `D` | rotate |
| `↑` / `W` | thrust |
| `Space` | fire |
| `Shift` / `0` | pulse |
| `P` / `Esc` | pause, then `R` restart or `Q` quit |
| `X` | sound on/off |

Your best score in each mode is saved in your browser.
